# Character bible — TF_TRADER_01

The canonical definition of the Trade Fix Radio visual performer. This document is
**authoritative**: where a painted plate, a rig or an animation disagrees with it, the plate,
rig or animation is wrong.

Companion drawings: `visual/references/character/TF_TRADER_01_proportions.svg` (construction,
proportions, rig joints) and `..._seated_geometry.svg` (seated pose against desk geometry).

---

## 1. What this character is

He is the visual embodiment of the station, not an avatar bolted onto it. The single sentence
every decision in this document answers to:

> The trader has been locked in for hours, markets are moving, coffee is hot, music is playing,
> and he is still working.

Everything that reads as *locked in for hours* is in scope. Everything that reads as
performance, charm, celebration or exhaustion is out of it. He is not entertaining anyone. He
is working, and the stream happens to be pointed at him.

**He is experienced.** This one word resolves most ambiguous animation calls. An experienced
trader seeing a breakout leans in and checks a second timeframe; he does not throw his hands
up. An experienced trader at 03:00 rolls a shoulder and re-focuses; he does not slump. When a
motion could be read two ways, the correct reading is always the one a disciplined
professional would recognise in himself.

## 2. Identity — frozen

These are not style preferences. They are the identity, and changing any of them produces a
different character.

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Internal name | `TF_TRADER_01` |
| Sex | Male |
| Ethnicity | White |
| Visual age target | **35–40**, reading closer to 37 |
| Height | **1 830 mm** |
| Build | Lean / athletic. Not heavy, not slight, **not bodybuilder** |
| Hair | Short, textured, **brown to dark blond**; natural part, slightly grown out, not styled today |
| Facial hair | **Short beard / heavy stubble**, 4–8 mm, evenly grown, trimmed neckline |
| Eyes | **Brown to hazel**, realistic scale and shape |
| Skin | Fair, warm-neutral undertone; slightly weathered but healthy |
| Register | Mature, disciplined, serious, focused. Attractive but **not model-like** |

### The three failure modes

| Failure | What it looks like | Why it is fatal |
|---|---|---|
| **Too young** | Large eyes, smooth jaw, unlined skin, soft neck | He becomes a student, and the entire premise — a professional who has done this for fifteen years — collapses |
| **Too stylised** | Anime eye proportions, chibi head ratio, cel-shaded blocking, exaggerated silhouette | The brief rules out cartoon, chibi, cel-shaded game character and generic VTuber by name |
| **Too photoreal** | Pore-level skin, photographic eye rendering | Also ruled out by name, and photoreal sits in an uncanny valley that twenty-four hours of looping movement makes worse, not better |

The target is the narrow band between them: **premium anime / illustrated realism** — realistic
proportion and anatomy, illustrated rendering. Realistic structure, painted surface.

### Age, made specific enough to paint

"35–40" is not a paintable instruction. These are:

- **Under-eye.** Visible but shallow tear-trough shadow. Faint lower-lid line. No bags, no
  puffiness, no bruising. The look of someone who sleeps six hours, not someone who is ill.
- **Brow and glabella.** One faint vertical crease between the brows, present at neutral and
  deepening on concentration. This single line does more age work than anything else.
- **Nasolabial.** Present at rest, soft, not a fold.
- **Jaw and neck.** Defined mandible with a clear gonial angle. Visible sternocleidomastoid
  under skin at head turns. No jowl, no softening under the chin.
- **Forehead.** Two faint horizontal lines on brow raise only. Smooth at neutral.
- **Crow's feet.** Barely present at neutral; appear on squint and on the confident smirk.
- **Hairline.** Very slight temple recession. Not thinning, not receding.
- **Hands.** Visible tendon and vein structure on the back of the hand. This is a strong age
  cue and the CAM 6 hands composition is where it reads.

What must **not** appear: deep wrinkles, grey hair, sunken cheeks, grey or sallow skin, sweat,
red eyes, visible distress. The brief's instruction is exact — *intense, not sick or exhausted*.

## 3. Face — the identity lock

The face is the only asset with zero tolerance for variation. Nine reference views must depict
one man. The mechanism is this section: a fixed set of measurable relationships that a
candidate plate either satisfies or does not.

Expressed in head units (**HU = 240 mm**, head height crown to chin) and as proportions of
head width (**HW = 155 mm**).

### Construction

| Landmark | Position | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|
| Head height (crown → chin) | 1.000 HU | — |
| Head width at zygomatic | 0.646 HU (155 mm) | ±2 % |
| Eye line from crown | 0.470 HU | ±0.01 |
| Inner canthus separation | 0.210 HU | ±0.008 |
| Single eye aperture width | 0.185 HU | ±0.008 |
| Eye aperture height / width | **0.34** | ±0.02 |
| Nose base from crown | 0.660 HU | ±0.012 |
| Nose width at alae | 0.165 HU | ±0.008 |
| Mouth line from crown | 0.775 HU | ±0.012 |
| Mouth width, relaxed | 0.215 HU | ±0.010 |
| Chin width at gonion | 0.415 HU | ±0.012 |
| Bizygomatic : bigonial | **1.00 : 0.84** | ±0.02 |
| Brow above upper lid margin | 0.055 HU | ±0.006 |
| Ear top / bottom | brow line / nose base | ±0.015 |

The **eye aperture ratio of 0.34** is the single most important number here. Anime drift shows
up in the eyes before anywhere else, and above roughly 0.42 he stops reading as 37 years old
no matter what the rest of the face does.

### Features

- **Brow ridge.** Present and masculine, casting a soft shadow into the orbit. Not heavy.
- **Eyes.** Almond, slight downward outer canthal tilt (2–4°) — this reads as seriousness
  rather than fatigue. Upper lid covers the top 15 % of the iris at neutral. Visible lash line,
  no rendered lashes. Iris brown-to-hazel with a warm mid-ring; specular highlight **from the
  monitors**, not from an invented key.
- **Nose.** Straight bridge, slight dorsal definition, defined tip, no upturn.
- **Mouth.** Medium lips, flat relaxed line, very slight natural downturn at the corners.
- **Beard.** Jawline-following, trimmed neckline two finger-widths above the Adam's apple.
  Moustache connected. Slightly denser on the chin. Painted as value mass with edge texture,
  never as individual strands.
- **Hair.** Short back and sides, 40–60 mm on top, textured and separated into irregular
  clumps. Natural left part. Two or three strands out of place at all times — a deliberate
  signature, consistent across all views. Base brown `#4a3a2c` → `#6b543c`, sun-lifted tips
  toward dark blond `#8a6f4a`.
- **Ears.** Normal, and **mostly occluded by the headphones** in every camera except CAM 2.

### Expression set — restrained, six only

| State | Brow | Eyes | Mouth | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `neutral_focus` | Level | Open, lids at 15 % | Flat | Default. The majority of all screen time. |
| `concentrated` | Drawn slightly in and down; glabellar line deepens | Narrowed 10–15 % | Flat, lips lightly compressed | `ANALYZING`, chart inspection |
| `slight_frown` | Inner brows down | Normal | Corners down 3 % | An adverse move |
| `subtle_concern` | Inner brows up and together | Widened 5 % | Slightly parted | A violent move; the most emotion he ever shows |
| `confident_smirk` | Level, one brow up 2 % | Faint crow's feet | **One** corner up 6 % | A thesis confirmed. Rare — see the cap below |
| `tired_focus` | Level | Lids at 25 %, blink rate up | Flat | Late in the fatigue cycle |

Hard caps, because restraint is the requirement and not the aspiration:

- `confident_smirk` and `subtle_concern` combined: **at most 2 % of runtime**.
- `confident_smirk`: **never during an adverse move**, regardless of what the scheduler wants.
- No expression exceeds the amplitudes above. There is no "stronger" version of any of them.
- **Forbidden entirely:** open-mouth smile, teeth, laughter, shock, anger, sadness, any
  anime-convention overlay (sweat drop, vein, blush, cross-pupil).

He is a man who has seen this before. His face says so.

## 4. Master model — layer taxonomy

ADR-13 commits to one master layered model. This is its structure, and it is fixed: the rig,
the exporter and the renderer all address layers by these names.

```
TF_TRADER_01.master
├── 00_construction            (guides; never exported)
├── 10_body
│   ├── 11_torso               ├── 12_neck
│   ├── 13_arm_L_upper         ├── 14_arm_L_fore      ├── 15_hand_L
│   ├── 16_arm_R_upper         ├── 17_arm_R_fore      ├── 18_hand_R
│   └── 19_legs_seated
├── 20_head
│   ├── 21_skull               ├── 22_face_base
│   ├── 23_brow_L / 23_brow_R
│   ├── 24_eye_L_{sclera,iris,pupil,spec}
│   ├── 24_eye_R_{sclera,iris,pupil,spec}
│   ├── 25_lid_upper_L/R       ├── 25_lid_lower_L/R
│   ├── 26_nose                ├── 27_mouth_{upper,lower,inner}
│   ├── 28_beard               └── 29_ear_L/R
├── 30_hair
│   ├── 31_hair_back  ├── 32_hair_mass  ├── 33_hair_fringe  └── 34_hair_strays
├── 40_outfit
│   ├── 41_shirt      ├── 42_overlayer  ├── 43_trousers
│   ├── 44_sneakers   └── 45_brand_mark
├── 50_accessories
│   ├── 51_headphones_band  ├── 52_cup_L  ├── 53_cup_R
│   ├── 54_cable            ├── 55_watch  └── 56_pendant
└── 60_lighting               (composited at runtime, not painted in)
    ├── 61_monitor_spill  ├── 62_amber_key  ├── 63_rim  └── 64_occlusion
```

Four rules about this tree:

1. **`20_head` is painted once.** Every camera uses the same head layers under a different
   projection. A per-camera head repaint is the exact failure the identity lock exists to stop.
2. **`30_hair` is four layers, not one**, so `34_hair_strays` can move independently and
   `33_hair_fringe` can respond to head rotation. A single hair layer makes hair movement
   impossible, which the brief asks for where supported.
3. **`60_lighting` is never painted into the plates.** Monitor spill and amber key are
   composited at runtime so they can modulate with market state and time. Baking them makes
   the lighting response in `OFFICE_BIBLE.md` §5 unimplementable.
4. **`52_cup_L` / `53_cup_R` are separate from the band** so an earcup adjustment deforms one
   cup without the band, and so one cup can be lifted off the ear.

## 5. Acceptance checklist — the identity gate

Every candidate reference plate is checked against this list before it enters
`visual/references/character/`. **Any single failure rejects the plate.** No partial accepts,
because a plate that is 90 % right is how a second face enters the project.

**Structure** — measure, do not eyeball:

- [ ] Eye aperture height ÷ width is 0.34 ± 0.02
- [ ] Inner canthus separation is 0.210 ± 0.008 HU
- [ ] Bizygomatic : bigonial is 1.00 : 0.84 ± 0.02
- [ ] Eye line at 0.470 ± 0.01 HU from crown
- [ ] Mouth line at 0.775 ± 0.012 HU from crown
- [ ] Head is 1/7.6 of standing height

**Identity** — present and consistent with the accepted master portrait:

- [ ] Single faint glabellar line at neutral
- [ ] Shallow tear-trough shadow, no bags
- [ ] Beard 4–8 mm, neckline trimmed, moustache connected
- [ ] 2–3 stray hairs, in the same region as the master
- [ ] Slight temple recession, no thinning
- [ ] Outer canthal tilt downward 2–4°
- [ ] Iris brown-to-hazel with warm mid-ring

**Register:**

- [ ] Reads 35–40 to a reviewer who has not seen the spec
- [ ] Reads as a professional trader, not a model, student or gamer
- [ ] No cartoon, chibi, cel-shaded or generic-VTuber signal
- [ ] No photoreal skin or eye rendering

**Palette** — against `frontend/tailwind.config.js`, which is the project's written art
direction and not a suggestion:

- [ ] Clothing blacks within the `ink` scale (`#07080a`–`#232935`)
- [ ] Gold only where §6 permits it, and within `#8a6f1c`–`#f0e0a6`
- [ ] Gold occupies **under 2 %** of frame area
- [ ] No colour outside the office palette in `OFFICE_BIBLE.md` §2

**Technical:**

- [ ] Layer names match §4 exactly
- [ ] Lighting layers separate and unbaked
- [ ] Transparent background, no shadow baked onto the ground
- [ ] 300 dpi at 1.5× final output size

## 6. Outfit

Premium, black, and almost entirely unbranded. The gold restraint rule from the palette file
applies to him as literally as to the console: **gold marks one thing at a time.**

| Slot | Specification |
|---|---|
| Base | Dark charcoal crew tee, `#151922`. Visible at the neck and under the open overlayer. |
| Overlayer | **Fitted black overshirt** — shirt-jacket cut, structured shoulder, straight hem, worn open, sleeves pushed to mid-forearm. `#0c0e12`, matte, subtle woven texture. |
| Trousers | Relaxed black tapered technical trousers, `#101319`. One low-profile thigh pocket. No webbing, no straps, no visible tactical hardware. |
| Footwear | Minimal black leather sneakers, `#07080a`, gum-free black sole. Barely visible — CAM 5 and CAM 6 only. |
| Watch | Black DLC case, 40 mm, black dial, **brushed-gold hands and indices only**, black rubber strap. **Left wrist.** |
| Pendant | Thin brushed-gold chain, short, usually under the tee. The small Trade Fix bar pendant shows **only** when he leans forward. |
| Branding | A single gold-foil Trade Fix mark, ~22 mm, left chest of the overlayer. Nothing else on him. |

**The overshirt over a hoodie, deliberately.** The brief allows either. The overshirt reads
*disciplined professional*; a hood reads *student at 2 a.m.*, which is the archetype this
character must be distinguishable from. The structured shoulder also gives the shoulder-shift
and roll motions a readable silhouette, which a soft hood absorbs. **Sleeves pushed to
mid-forearm** is load-bearing rather than cosmetic: the brief requires forearms visible enough
for desk motions, and it is what makes the watch and the hand tendon structure readable.

### Headphones — `TF-HP-01`

The strongest silhouette element and the most-seen prop. Specified tightly because they appear
in every camera and are touched by four separate movements.

| Part | Specification |
|---|---|
| Form | Closed-back circumaural, oval cups, 95 × 78 mm |
| Headband | Matte black aluminium, 14 mm, exposed slider arms |
| Cups | Matte black, `#0c0e12`; a **1.2 mm brushed-gold ring** at the cup-to-arm pivot |
| Logo | Gold Trade Fix mark, 14 mm, outer face of **each** cup |
| Pads | Black protein leather, visible stitch, compressed where they meet the head |
| Cable | Braided black, **left cup**, falling behind the shoulder to the desk |
| Rendering | Matte body with one controlled specular along the top of the band. No chrome, no bloom. |

The gold rings and cup marks are the one place gold is permitted to appear twice, because they
are a single symmetric object read as one element.

Proportions for a 1 830 mm male at 7.6 head units. Drawn in

| Wrist height | 880 |
| Upper arm | 365 |
| Forearm | 270 |
| Hand | 195 |

### Seated — the canonical working pose

Seat pan at **460 mm**, desk surface at **735 mm**.

| Measure | mm from floor |
|---|---|
| Seat pan | 460 |
| Thigh top (clearance) | 625 |
| **Elbow** | **700** |
| Desk surface | 735 |
| Shoulder | 1 080 |
| Chin | 1 215 |
| **Eye** | **1 295** |
| Crown | 1 455 |

Two consequences that have to be right or every desk motion looks wrong:

- **Elbow at 700 mm sits 35 mm below a 735 mm desk.** Forearms therefore rest *on* the desk
  from mid-forearm forward, with a slight upward angle from elbow to wrist. If the rig places
  the elbow above the surface, every typing and mouse motion will read as hovering.
- **Eye at 1 295 mm against a main-monitor centre at 1 130 mm** means he looks **14.2° down** at
  the primary chart — correct ergonomics, and the reason the gaze system in `MOTION_LIBRARY.md`
  §7 carries a negative default pitch. The monitors' top bezel lands at 1 298, level with his
  eye line, which is what forces every front camera to be elevated (`CAMERA_PLAN.md` §1).

Posture: upright with a 4–6° forward lean at the thoracic spine. Shoulders level and relaxed,
not squared. Weight slightly on the forearms. **He is leaning in a little, always** — that is
what hours of work looks like, and it is what makes the explicit `lean_forward` motion read as
a change rather than as a correction.

## 8. Rig

Joints required for the motion library, with their 2D degrees of freedom. The deformation
method is per-layer mesh deformation driven by this skeleton — see `MOTION_LIBRARY.md` §8.

| Chain | Joints | DoF each | Drives |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spine | `pelvis`, `spine_01`, `spine_02`, `chest` | rot Z, trans Y | Breathing, posture shift, lean |
| Neck | `neck`, `head` | rot Z, yaw proxy, trans XY | Head turn, tilt, drift, nod |
| Shoulders | `clavicle_L/R`, `shoulder_L/R` | rot Z, trans XY | Shoulder shift, roll, breathing lift |
| Arms | `elbow_L/R`, `wrist_L/R` | rot Z | Reach, typing, mouse |
| Hands | `hand_L/R` + 5 finger chains × 2 joints | rot Z | Typing, grip, finger tap |
| Face — eyes | `eye_L/R` | pitch, yaw (gaze) | Gaze targeting, micro-saccade |
| Face — lids | `lid_upper_L/R`, `lid_lower_L/R` | 0→1 close | Blink, squint, tired lids |
| Face — brows | `brow_in_L/R`, `brow_out_L/R` | trans Y, rot Z | All six expressions |
| Face — mouth | `jaw`, `mouth_corner_L/R`, `lip_upper`, `lip_lower` | trans XY | Jaw movement, smirk, compression |
| Face — cheeks | `cheek_L/R` | trans Y | Squint support, smirk support |
| Props | `hp_band`, `hp_cup_L/R`, `hp_cable` | rot Z, trans XY | Four headphone movements |

Non-negotiable: **`eye_L` and `eye_R` take a world-space gaze target, not a rotation.** The
gaze system aims at anchors declared in the blockout, so that "look at the left monitor"
resolves to the actual left monitor rather than to a hand-tuned angle per camera. Hand-tuned
gaze angles are how eyes end up looking *through* monitors, which the brief lists as a
constraint violation.

### Painting order, and why it is an order

1. **View 1 alone.** Iterate until §5 passes. Nothing else proceeds.
2. Views 4 and 5, with view 1 supplied as an image reference. Check §5 against view 1, not
   against the spec in isolation — the question is *"is this the same man"*, not *"is this a
   good face"*.
3. Views 2 and 3. Profiles are where identity drifts most, so they come after the easier
   three-quarters have confirmed the reference transfers.
4. View 6, then 8. Proportion before pose.
5. View 7, then 9.

If a later view cannot be made to match view 1, **view 1 is not re-opened.** The later view is
repainted. The master does not move once accepted, and that is the whole mechanism.

## 10. Long-term variation — what may drift and what may not

Over weeks, the brief asks for slow variation without identity change. The line is drawn here.

**May vary:** posture set in use, lean amount, which monitor he favours, sleeve-push height,
whether the pendant is visible, coffee frequency, stray-hair arrangement within the same
region, mug position, notebook page, expression distribution within §3's caps.

**May never vary:** any §2 identity attribute, any §3 construction measurement, the six
expressions and their amplitudes, the outfit slots, `TF-HP-01`, the watch on the left wrist,
the single chest mark, the palette.

A viewer returning after a month must recognise him instantly. Everything in the first list is
how the stream stays interesting; everything in the second is why it is still *his* stream.
